Game Dev Starter Guide

A beginner-friendly introduction to making games.

Start with an idea

Choose a small game concept, such as a platformer, puzzle, or simple arcade game. Keep the first version small so you can finish it.

Explore game-making tools

Look into a game engine that fits your goals, then try a beginner tutorial and make a tiny playable prototype.

Learn as you build

Game development combines design, art, sound, and programming. Pick one small feature to learn at a time and keep notes on what you discover.
